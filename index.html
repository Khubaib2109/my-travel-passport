Welcome to My Travel Passport

Quick guide

This globe shows your travel history. Add trips manually or paste/import a spreadsheet, then use the globe and the trip list to explore routes and play them back in chronological order. It is free to use, but location accuracy and geocoding are not guaranteed.

Rotate and zoomDrag the globe to rotate it and use your mouse wheel or trackpad to zoom in and out.
Add or import tripsUse “Add a trip” for one route, or “Bulk import” to paste an Excel table or upload CSV/XLSX.
Focus a routeClick any trip card to centre that route on the globe and inspect the path more closely.
Play your timelineUse “Play timeline” to animate your routes in date order. Adjust playback speed at the bottom right.
